Similar to the Coverage ID generating upon activating an account automatically when registering. They are able to click a link to view the COE ID. They are asked if they read the approval ID (yes or no radio buttons) and if they'd like a copy of the ID mailed (yes or no radio buttons). <del>There is also a complete button.</del>  “Complete” button is not needed per Shaun’s comment.</p> <p>There is also an additional screen that the user gets taken to after the initially select the Corporate Officer Exclusion Election Link in the View or Update Account area of the old portal. But you can also see the user taken to the screen at 13 seconds into the video I already attached showing how everything works in the old portal.</p> <p>When they select complete they are taken to the View Or Update area of the account. They can then go back in and try to take the election again but get a message saying that they have already taken the election. I was reviewing that messaging when retesting <a href="https://dwd-wi.atlassian.net/browse/UIEP-3370" title="smart-link" class="external-link" rel="nofollow noreferrer">https://dwd-wi.atlassian.net/browse/UIEP-3370</a> </p> <p>In the new portal when they submit, nothing happens.
