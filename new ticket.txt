Root Cause: When returning to a Corporation registration via Registration Number + FEIN, the Ownership step was only showing the President slot. Vice President, Secretary, and Treasurer were missing. This happened because the restore logic was calling Officers.Clear() first, wiping all 4 predefined officer slots, and then only adding back officers that had data saved in the database. Since only President was filled in the original submission, the other 3 slots were lost.

Fix: Updated CorporationOwnershipForm.razor in the RestoreFromContinueData() method. Instead of clearing all slots and re-adding only saved ones, the fix merges saved data into the existing predefined slots. All 4 officer roles (President, Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer) now always appear on the update path. President is pre-populated with saved data and the remaining slots are available as empty editable fields.

Fixed and verified locally. ✅
