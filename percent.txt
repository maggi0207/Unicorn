<title>[UIEP-3744] Incorrect Line 5 Tax Rate Under With Exclusion Column on Corporate Officer Exclusion Worksheet</title>
<link>https://dwd-wi.atlassian.net/browse/UIEP-3744</link>
<project id="10108" key="UIEP">UI Employer Portal</project>
<description><p>ER 376896-000-5</p> <p>On the Corporate Officer Exclusion Reasonableness Worksheet in the portal the Your 2026 WI UI Tax Rate Figure (line 5) is incorrect on the With Exclusion Column. On account 376896-000-5 it reads as 305.00% but, on this account, it should be 3.05%. </p> <p>Line 5 under both columns should be the same and it should be whatever the employer's actually assigned tax rate is for the year in question. </p> <p>I've attached a screenshot.</p> <p>Note: I should have entered 0.00 in the fillable field box in the With Exclusion column but that has no effect on the issue being reported. </p> <p>I chose other functions as the portal area because it was the closest correct area in my opinion. </p> <p><span class="image-wrap" style=""><img src="https://dwd-wi.atlassian.net/rest/api/3/attachment/content/137609" alt="image-20261008-193306.png" width="742" style="border: 0px solid black" /></span></p> <p>Steps: Go to the Corporate Officer Exclusion Reasonableness Worksheet screen in the View &amp; Manage Account area for an employer that is eligible for the Corporate Officer Exclusion (COE) and review line 5 of the worksheet to ensure that the correct tax rate is being displayed.</p> <p>The easiest way to get an account to do this with is to submit a registration for a corporation that indicates that they have met a liability condition so that the account will activate. That account should be eligible for the COE because they will just have been made subject. </p></description>
